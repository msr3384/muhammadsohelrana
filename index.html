👋 About Me

I am a mathematician and data scientist interested in computational methods that combine geometry, probability, statistics, and machine learning. My work emphasizes optimal transport and Wasserstein geometry, particularly for manifold learning, dimensionality reduction, distance recovery, clustering, and scientific data analysis.

🔬 Research Interests

OT

Optimal Transport

Wasserstein metrics, unbalanced optimal transport, Hellinger–Kantorovich geometry, and computational optimal transport.

ML

Machine Learning

Geometric learning, clustering, classification, structure-preserving methods, and interpretable computational models.

DR

Dimensionality Reduction

Manifold learning, neighbor embeddings, nonlinear representations, and visualization of complex data.

DM

Distance-Matrix Recovery

Low-rank recovery, Nyström approximation, CUR-based methods, and efficient reconstruction from limited measurements.

FL

Federated Learning

Privacy-aware learning, self-adaptive clients, distributed optimization, and heterogeneous data.

SD

Scientific Data Analysis

Computational methods for imaging, healthcare, network, biological, and geospatial datasets.
