Connection to My Academic Career

My banking experience continues to influence my work as an educator and researcher. Working directly with clients taught me how to explain complicated procedures in a clear and accessible way. This skill is especially valuable when teaching mathematical and statistical concepts to students with different levels of preparation.

The need for accuracy, confidentiality, documentation, and careful review in banking also strengthened my attention to detail. These habits support my work in statistical analysis, research writing, course preparation, assessment, and academic administration.

Finally, collaborating with colleagues across different operational units developed my teamwork and professional communication skills, which now support my interactions with students, faculty members, researchers, and institutional staff.
