Teaching Approach

My goal is to create an inclusive and supportive classroom in which students can develop mathematical confidence through guided practice, discussion, problem solving, and meaningful applications.

“Mathematics is the language with which God has written the universe.” — Galileo Galilei

Teaching Philosophy

My teaching philosophy is centered on clarity, engagement, inclusivity, and helping students develop lasting mathematical understanding.

01

Clarity and Structure

I explain concepts step by step, connect new ideas to prerequisite knowledge, and provide organized examples that help students understand both procedures and underlying reasoning.

02

Active Learning

I encourage students to participate through guided practice, peer discussion, problem-solving activities, questions, and opportunities to explain their reasoning.

03

Inclusive Support

I aim to create a respectful environment where students from different backgrounds and levels of preparation feel comfortable asking questions and developing confidence.
